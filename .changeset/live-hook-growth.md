`useLiveSubscription` gives `fetchingMore`, true while a growth runs, and `fetchMore()` adds a page of the first page's size. A call during a growth is ignored, and a growth that fails goes to `error` and `onError` while the list goes on as it was. A message keeps the object of every row it did not change, so a row component in `memo` renders only the rows that did.
