UltimaBase is a backend written in TypeScript. You declare models, their
fields and who may read and write them; it serves them over GraphQL or REST,
on PostgreSQL or in memory. A small kernel holds the models, the queries and
the access rules, and every other feature is a plugin: HTTP, GraphQL, REST,
sign-in, files, realtime, the databases.

```npm
npm create ultimabase@latest my-app
```
