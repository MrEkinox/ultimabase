`@ultimabase/stripe` keeps what the application sells in its code: products,
their prices and the credits a payment gives. The deploy script writes them
to [Stripe](https://stripe.com), with the customer portal's configuration.
Users pay on Stripe's Checkout and manage their subscriptions on its portal;
Stripe's events keep the application's billing models in step.

```npm
npm install @ultimabase/stripe
```

## The catalog

A product is a subscription, with its recurring prices by name, or a product
paid once, with one price. Either gives credits with `gives`: a purchase
once, a subscription's price each paid period. An amount is an integer in
the currency's smallest unit: `9_90` is 9.90 EUR.

- A key holds letters, digits, `-` and `_`; a price's lookup key in Stripe is
  `<product>.<price>`, as `pro.monthly`, or the product's key for a price
  paid once.
- A subscription has one price per interval: the customer portal moves a
  subscription between them.
- `everyCount` bills every few intervals, up to three years.

Each line names a change, as `create the price pro.quarterly: 27.00 EUR
every 3 months`; `yarn deploy --dry-run` prints them and changes nothing.

- A run changes only what differs: a second run prints `up to date`.
- Stripe never changes a price's amount or interval: a new price replaces it,
  takes its lookup key, and the old one is archived. Subscriptions on the old
  price keep it.
- A product or price the catalog no longer lists is archived, never deleted.
- `taxBehavior` is `'inclusive'` unless set: the amounts include the taxes.
- A new Stripe account sells through Managed Payments, whose Checkout refuses
  a product with no `taxCode`, as `taxCode: 'txcd_10103000'`.
- The secret key's mode is printed: `sk_live_…` writes to the live account.

## The plugin

`stripePlugin` sells the catalog. It needs `httpPlugin`, for Stripe's
webhook, and `jobsPlugin`, whose jobs write Stripe's events to the models.

```npm
npm install @ultimabase/jobs
```

</include>

<include cwd meta='title="src/app.ts"'>
	../examples/stripe/src/app.ts#app

- `keys` holds the secret key and the webhook's signing secret. Without
  them, the application starts, every call that needs Stripe throws
  `PAYMENT_UNAVAILABLE`, and the webhook answers 503.
- `customer` gives what Stripe shows of a payer, from its row: `email`,
  `name`, `locale`. Each payer has one Stripe customer, created at its first
  Checkout and closed when the payer's row is deleted. Stripe gets the
  profile again at a Checkout or a portal visit when it changed.
- At start, the plugin compares the catalog with Stripe. With
  `NODE_ENV=development` and a test key it applies it; otherwise it warns
  and lists the differences: the deploy script applies them.
- `webhookPath` is `/webhooks/stripe` unless set; `oneSubscription: true`
  refuses a second live subscription with `ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED`, and one made
  anyway, in the Dashboard or by a race, is canceled at once, its payment
  refunded, and logged as an error.

## The billing models

Four functions declare the models the plugin writes, each with its fields;
the application adds its own. Only the plugin writes them: a client's or
the server's write is refused. Reads follow the application's rules: here,
with `isOwner` from `@ultimabase/auth`, each user reads their own rows.
Deleting a payer, under its own rules, takes its rows with it.

<include cwd meta='title="src/models.ts"'>
	../examples/stripe/src/models.ts#models
</include>

The subscriptions' model, with its hook, is under
[React to changes](#react-to-changes).

- `defineSubscription`: `payer`, `stripeId`, `product`, `price`, `status`,
  `quantity`, `amount`, `every`, `everyCount`, `currentPeriodEnd`,
  `cancelAt`, `canceledAt`, `trialEnd`, `startedAt`, and the change the
  portal scheduled: `scheduledProduct`, `scheduledPrice`, `scheduledAt`.
- `definePurchase`: `payer`, `stripeId`, `product`, `quantity`, `amount`,
  `refunded`, `status` (`'paid'` or `'refunded'`), `paidAt`, `receiptUrl`.
- `defineBalance`: `owner`, `credit`, `amount`.
- `defineCreditHistory`: `owner`, `credit`, `change`, positive when given
  and negative when spent or taken back, `reason`, and `payment`, the
  Stripe payment or invoice that moved it.
- An application has at most one model of each. The purchases' payer, the
  balances' owner and the history's owner are one model, and a balance
  needs its history: the application does not start without it.

A page reads what a user pays from the row, not from the catalog: a
subscriber keeps the price they subscribed at after the catalog changed.

- `amount` is one period's price times the quantity, before discounts and
  taxes; `every` and `everyCount` are the interval as the catalog writes
  it.
- `status` is one of Stripe's: `incomplete`, `incomplete_expired`,
  `trialing`, `active`, `past_due`, `canceled`, `unpaid`, `paused`.
- The invoices stay on Stripe's portal, with their PDFs; a purchase's
  `receiptUrl` is Stripe's receipt page.

## Checkout and the portal

`stripe.checkout` opens Stripe's Checkout for a payer and returns its URL.
It closes the payer's Checkouts still open, so two tabs cannot both pay.

<include cwd meta='title="src/shop.ts"'>
	../examples/stripe/src/shop.ts#checkout
</include>

- `price` names one of a subscription's prices; a product with one price
  needs none.
- `quantity`, `trialDays`, `terms`, `promotionCodes` and `metadata` are
  optional.
- With `promotionCodes: true`, the payer can type a code created in Stripe.
  A purchase a code made free is recorded at zero and gives its credits.

The success page writes what the payer paid at once, before Stripe's event
arrives. It finds only the payer's own Checkout.

## The webhook

In Stripe, create an event destination for the application's URL, with
the path `/webhooks/stripe` and thin payloads, and give its signing secret
to `keys.webhook`.

- The plugin checks each event's signature on the raw body and its mode,
  stores it once, enqueues its job and answers at once. A forged event gets
  401; an event of the other mode, live or test, is dropped with a warning.
- The job reads the customer from Stripe and writes its subscriptions,
  purchases and credits. Events can come late or in any order: the rows
  follow Stripe, not the event.
- An hourly job runs the events still waiting, syncs again the customers
  whose period ended, and closes the customers of deleted payers. It also
  reads Stripe's list of events since its last run, and syncs each customer
  they changed that no sync read since: an event the webhook missed costs
  one sync within the hour.

## Credits

A payment adds what its product gives to the payer's balances, once:

- a purchase, times its quantity;
- a subscription, each paid invoice, for each period it bills, times the
  quantity: the first payment and each renewal. A yearly price gives its
  credits at once, for the year.
- A trial gives nothing until its first payment, and a prorated change
  nothing until the next renewal. A period that ended before the plugin
  read its invoice gives nothing: a `gives` added to a live price gives
  from the next renewal.

`stripe.grant` adds credits without a payment, and `stripe.spend` takes
them, each in the caller's transaction when there is one.

- A balance lacking the credits throws `NOT_ENOUGH_CREDITS`, with
  `{ credit, balance, needed }`, and changes nothing.
- A full refund or a lost dispute takes back what the payment gave; those
  already spent stay spent, and the plugin warns.
- Each move is a row of the history, with its `reason`: the
  application's, or the plugin's `purchase`, `subscription` and `refund`.

## React to changes

The models' hooks run on the plugin's writes, as on any other: a sync
writes a row only when Stripe changed it, so a hook runs once a change,
not once an event. Its `caller` is `master`.

</include>

- `afterCreate` and `afterUpdate` run in the sync's transaction: throwing
  undoes it.
- A mail or a call to another service waits for `afterCreateCommit` or
  `afterUpdateCommit`, which run once the write commits. See
  [Hooks](/docs/next/hooks).
- A history's `afterCreateCommit` sees each move of credits.

## Errors

| Code                  | When                                                         |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `PAYMENT_UNAVAILABLE` | The plugin has no keys, or Stripe does not answer.           |
| `ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED`  | `oneSubscription` and a Checkout of a second subscription.   |
| `NOT_SUBSCRIBED`      | A portal's `change` or `cancel` without a live subscription. |
| `NOT_ENOUGH_CREDITS`  | `stripe.spend` takes more than the balance holds.            |
| `NOT_FOUND`           | A payer that is no row, or `syncCheckout` of another payer.  |
